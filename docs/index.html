01 — Features

Built to disappear
until you need it.

/01

Global summon

One keystroke brings the window to the front of any app, on any desktop — even over full-screen video.

/02

Three assistants

Pick Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude on open, and swap between them instantly without a reload.

/03

Dial the transparency

A live opacity slider lets the window melt into your screen so you can read what's behind it.

/04

Vanishes on blur

Click away or hit Esc and it fades out. Your place, your session — always kept.

/05

Frameless & light

No chrome, no clutter. A single translucent pane that stays out of your way and out of your dock.

/06

Yours to keep

Open source, no account, no telemetry. Logins live in your own browser session, nowhere else.

02 — How it works

Three keystrokes
to a smarter desktop.

  1. 01

    Install & forget

    Download for your platform and open once. Halo tucks itself into the menu bar and waits.

  2. 02

    Press Option + Space

    From anywhere, the launcher fades in centered on your active screen. Choose your assistant.

  3. 03

    Ask, then dismiss

    Type your prompt. When you're done, click away — it disappears and your session is saved for next time.
